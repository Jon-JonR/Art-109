➴About me

Jon-Jon utilizes existing media identified throughout his life and incorporates them in a way that defines the significance of his work — the impact that media has on the core memories of a person. With clever innovation and technique, Jon-Jon is capable of alluding to diverse references in his work through video editing and 3D design. Jon-Jon’s purpose is to create familiarity in these references, some requiring common mutual memories of the audience, while others require specific recalls to past media.
