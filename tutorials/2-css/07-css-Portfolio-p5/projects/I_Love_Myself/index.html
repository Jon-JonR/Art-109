I Love Myself

Inspired by Kanye West's "All of the Lights," music video produced in 2011, this music video follows with the song, "I" by Kendrick Lamar which tackles on the idea of loving yourself during a time where suicide takes a toll on people's lives young or old, black or white. Visual lyrics, accompanied by flashing letters, gives a kind of feeling that despite being somewhat painful to see, gives off a feeling of being alive, similar to the kind of feeling one would get in a concert.
